# Research Bibliography and Verified References

The following sources were reviewed during development. Metadata below is based on source pages and bibliographic records, not reconstructed from memory. Use the original source when citing or applying the method.

## 1. Cultural public sphere and #JogjaOraDidol

Fahmi, U. (2019). Cultural public sphere: Tracking the Yogyakarta City policy agenda through the #JogjaOraDidol hashtag on Twitter. *Policy & Governance Review, 3*(1), 73–90. https://doi.org/10.30589/pgr.v3i1.123

- Methodological relevance: mixed-method combination of topic modelling, social network analysis, and discourse analysis.
- Analytical dimensions: structural, representational, and interactional.
- Case relevance: social-media discourse and local policy agenda in Yogyakarta.
- Source: https://journal.iapa.or.id/pgr/article/view/123
- Secondary thesis record on the related research: https://etd.repository.ugm.ac.id/penelitian/detail/162963

## 2. Mapping principal-agent relations in social media

Fahmi, U., Wibowo, C. P., & Yudanto, F. (2018). Mapping the principal-agent relation in social media: A data mining approach. *Jurnal Administrasi dan Kebijakan Publik, 3*(2), 158–178. https://doi.org/10.25077/jakp.3.2.158-178.2018

- Methodological relevance: interaction between government and society on social media, including account posts and citizens' mentions/replies; principal-agent framing.
- Source: https://jakp.fisip.unand.ac.id/index.php/jakp/article/view/28
- Additional record: https://garuda.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/documents/detail/1038853

## 3. Hybrid method: text mining and qualitative approach

Fahmi, U., Wibowo, C. P., & Yudanto, F. (2018). Hybrid method in revealing facts behind texts: A combination of text mining and qualitative approach. In *Proceedings of the 2018 Annual Conference of Asian Association for Public Administration: Reinventing Public Administration in a Globalized World—A Non-Western Perspective* (pp. 132–148). Atlantis Press. https://doi.org/10.2991/aapa-18.2018.14

- Methodological relevance: hybrid text-mining and qualitative approach for interpreting social-media soft data.
- Source: https://www.atlantis-press.com/proceedings/aapa-18/25896109

## 4. Additional reporting-systematics reference: Drone Emprit

Drone Emprit. (2026, November 6). *Sentimen publik terhadap salam tiga jari*. https://pers.droneemprit.id/sentimen-publik-terhadap-salam-tiga-jari/

- Used as a reference for report organization: conversation overview, temporal trends, sentiment/emotion, discourse clusters/narratives, platform comparison, engagement, actors, propagation, keywords/hashtags, top content, and synthesis.
- This is an organization reference only. The Skill does not claim to reproduce proprietary Drone Emprit algorithms, classifications, or analyses.

## Citation and method integrity
- Cite original publications for academic claims and methods.
- Do not claim a source validates an implementation unless the source and implementation have been compared.
- References guide research design; the bundled prototype does not implement every method in these papers.
- Topic modelling, SNA, discourse analysis, and hybrid methods are distinct components. Do not imply the prototype currently implements all of them.
