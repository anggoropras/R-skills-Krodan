# Digital Discourse & Policy Insight Report Template

This template combines quantitative descriptive reporting, evidence-based narrative interpretation, and policy-research synthesis. Use sections relevant to the research question and available data; never fabricate metrics for unavailable analyses.

## 1. Executive Summary
- Issue/case, observation period, dataset size, platforms
- 3–5 findings in the format: quantitative evidence → observed pattern → interpretation → implication
- Key limitation and confidence

## 3. Conversation Overview
Report total posts/mentions, unique authors, unique content, active days, mean daily volume, peak volume, and content repetition. State denominators and describe what volume can and cannot represent.

## 4. Temporal Dynamics & Event Context
- Daily/weekly volume trend and peak dates
- Change rates and concentration across peak periods
- Event timeline, when independently documented
- Distinguish temporal coincidence from causal claims

## 5. Sentiment Overview (only if method and data support it)
| Sentiment | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Positive | | |
| Neutral | | |
| Negative | | |
| Total | | 100% |

Report sentiment by platform, topic, actor, and time when appropriate. Define Net Sentiment Score if used: % positive minus % negative. Explain classifier, annotation protocol, validation metrics, and uncertainty. Do not report validated sentiment without human-labelled evaluation.

## 6. Sentiment Dynamics
Compare sentiment proportions across time; identify peaks, turning points, event associations, and alternative explanations. Use stacked trends or comparable visualizations.

## 7. Emotion Analysis (only if supported)
Define emotion categories and classifier/coding method. Report frequencies/shares, dominant emotion, temporal trends, platform/topic breakdowns, and evidence excerpts. Do not confuse emotion with sentiment.

## 8. Issue & Topic Distribution
| Rank | Topic/theme | Count | Share of posts or mentions | Change |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| | | | | |

State whether classification is keyword dictionary, human coding, statistical topic model, or another method. Explain multi-label counting and validate themes with close reading.

## 9. Narrative & Discourse Analysis
For each major narrative:
- Narrative label and claim
- Supporting volume/percentage and time period
- Actors/platforms carrying it
- Framing and keywords
- Counter-narrative/negative cases
- Short evidence excerpts with source IDs/links
- Interpretation, uncertainty and alternative explanation

## 10. Actor Composition & Network Mapping
- Actor-category distribution and contribution
- Reply/mention/repost network definition (nodes, edges, direction, weights)
- Network metrics and cluster/community results only if implemented
- Top contributors, receivers, connectors, and interaction patterns
- Do not equate activity/centrality with influence, authority, coordination, or representativeness.

## 11. Platform Comparison
| Platform | Volume | Sentiment (if validated) | Top themes | Engagement | Dominant narrative |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|

Compare platform-specific content, sentiment/emotion, actors, engagement, and narratives. Do not infer cross-platform migration without linked evidence and temporal support.

## 12. Engagement & Content Performance
Report likes, comments, shares, total engagement, mean/median per post, distribution, top content/accounts, concentration, and platform/topic comparison. Engagement totals are not engagement rates without reach/impression denominators.

## 13. Cross-platform Diffusion (optional; requires evidence)
Document first appearance, subsequent platforms, time lag, related content/URLs/hashtags, amplification patterns, and uncertainty. Volume sequence alone does not establish diffusion.

## 14. Keywords, Hashtags & Framing
Provide term/hashtag frequencies, context, co-occurrence or network results where calculated, semantic framing, and representative evidence. Frequency is not importance by itself.

## 15. Top Content & Evidence
| Rank | Post ID/link | Platform/date | Content summary | Metric | Narrative/theme |
|---:|---|---|---|---|

Define ranking criteria and preserve traceability. Protect personal data and quote only what is necessary.

## 16. Integrated Insights & Policy Implications
For each insight use:
**Claim → quantitative evidence (with denominator/time) → observed pattern → narrative/context → actor/platform → implication → confidence/limitation.**
Tie recommendations to specific findings. Separate evidence, inference, hypothesis, and recommendation.

## 17. Limitations, Ethics & Confidence
Discuss sample size/representativeness, platform coverage, collection/query bias, missingness, repeated content, classifier validity, language/cultural context, privacy, and uncertainty. Assign High/Medium/Low confidence with rationale where useful.

## 18. Reproducibility Appendix
Data inventory, schema, field mapping, preprocessing, filters, code/version, parameters, evaluation results, run date, output artifacts, and annotation protocol.
