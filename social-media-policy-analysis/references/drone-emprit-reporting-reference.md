## Elements used as reporting inspiration
The public report's organization illustrates a narrative-driven digital discourse report that moves beyond aggregate counts. Elements reflected in this Skill's report template:
- Overall issue framing and method/platform/time scope
- Conversation volume and temporal peaks with event context
- Sentiment overview and sentiment change
- Emotion categories and triggers
- Actor/discourse clusters and cluster narratives
- Platform-specific comparison
- Issue/topic distribution and engagement
- Cross-platform spread/diffusion
- Influencer/actor activity, keywords/hashtags, and top content
- Synthesis/conclusion

## Adaptation principles
- This is a reference for report organization, not a reproduction of Drone Emprit's proprietary methods or analysis.
- Metrics must be calculated from the available dataset and documented methods.
- Sentiment/emotion results require method disclosure and validation appropriate to the task.
- Cluster, network, influencer, and diffusion claims must be supported by implemented metrics and traceable evidence.
- Do not infer causality from coincident peaks, or public representativeness from social-media volume.
- Use only sections supported by the data; mark unavailable analyses as not assessed rather than filling them with guesses.
